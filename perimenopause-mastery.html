The business case

A transition most workplaces have no plan for.

For many women, perimenopause symptoms land in the years of peak experience, seniority and responsibility. It goes well beyond hot flushes; sleep, concentration, mood, confidence and energy can all change, and the women living through it often have no language for it at work, no policy to point to, and a GP appointment that may not go the way they hope.

EDI relevance

Menopause is a health equity issue.

CIPD found the career impact is greater for women with a disability or long-term condition, and for ethnic minority women (2).

This talk helps close the gap between stated inclusion commitments and the lived experience of women in their forties and fifties.

Educational, not medical advice. Treatment decisions, including HRT, are best made with a clinician.
