The 4 Seasons of Your Cycle

A map of how your hormones move across the month, and how to work with each season.
Vibrant Leader
Academy
Information, not instructions.
About 28 days is an average, and at your age an irregular cycle is completely normal. These seasons are a map, not a timetable. They tell you how you might feel, never what you are allowed to do, achieve or decide.
Your hormones across the month
Winter
MENSTRUATION
EnergyLow
BodyHormones at their lowest. Often the most tired week.
Mind"Everything feels like a mountain." Recovery, not laziness.
SchoolLow-stakes: review, organise, flashcards.
MoveGentle movement eases cramps. Rest is fine.
SocialRunning near empty. Saying no to plans is fine.
Spring
FOLLICULAR
EnergyRising
BodyOestrogen climbing. Energy and motivation rise.
Mind"I can handle this." Optimistic and capable.
SchoolFront-load: start essays, tackle hard topics.
MoveGood for harder training or a new sport.
SocialOpening up. Keen to join in and speak up.
Summer
OVULATION
EnergyPeak
BodyOestrogen peaks, brief testosterone rise. Confidence high.
Mind"Ready to be seen." Magnetic and persuasive.
SchoolPresentations, debates, interviews.
MoveStrong for competition. Warm up well.
SocialFull charge. The life of the group.
Autumn
LUTEAL
EnergyDipping
BodyOestrogen drops. The brain starts hunting for flaws.
MindInner Critic and Mirror Lie. Not facts: "look again in 5 days."
SchoolEdit, proofread and finish, rather than start.
MoveMaintenance, not gains. Keep it steady.
SocialPatience runs thin. Friendship friction flares.
REMEMBER: pain or low mood that wrecks your month is not something to just put up with. It is worth talking to a parent, the school nurse or a doctor.
